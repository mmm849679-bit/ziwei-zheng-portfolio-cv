Human–Computer Interaction · Design Informatics

Ziwei Zheng

Public Art · Physical Making · Interactive Media

I’m a Design Informatics master’s student at the University of Edinburgh with a background in Public Art. Through user-centred research and hands-on prototyping, I explore how everyday objects, physical spaces and digital media shape human experience.

My work spans interactive installations, speculative design and participatory urban planning.

Research Interests

My research interests lie in Human–Computer Interaction, particularly tangible and embodied interaction, multisensory experiences, and participatory and speculative design. I explore how physical objects, spaces and digital media can support personal memory, emotional engagement and public reflection on the social and environmental implications of technology.

Current Research Projects

Ongoing · Group Project

The Trembling “Cloud” — Data Cities

Exploring how a multisensory installation can make the physical impacts of AI infrastructure perceptible through sound, vibration and proximity-based interaction.

Ongoing · HCI Study

Everyday Keepsakes & Digital Memory

Investigating how students preserve meaningful keepsakes and create digital memory pages through semi-structured interviews and think-aloud usability evaluation.

Selected Design Projects

Second Breath — Tangible Interaction & Immersive Experience

Developed an immersive nature prototype informed by interviews and personas, using NFC-tagged natural objects to trigger projected scenes and sound.

Urban Blocks — XR for Participatory Urban Planning

Designed an XR platform concept for collaborative urban planning, translating stakeholder needs into modular spaces, role-based workflows and interface concepts.

The Fate of Pure City — Speculative Design & Data Ethics

Explored how environmental data could shape social governance through fictional interfaces, miniature sets and stop-motion storytelling, addressing privacy, agency and inequality.

City as Interface — User Research & Service Design

Translated interviews, personas and content analysis into a reusable video-production workflow and a map-based local discovery service concept.
